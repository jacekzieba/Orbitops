# Baltic dashboard map

Country polygons: Natural Earth 1:50m Admin 0 Countries, public domain.
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The source geometry is clipped to longitude 8–34°E and latitude 50.8–62.3°N, rounded to 0.001°. Browser rendering uses a common Mercator projection with equal X/Y projection scale. Country outlines and the four city coordinates are geographical; maritime/road routes, weather extent and animation are illustrative demo overlays. The wind mask excludes land polygons. Scale bar is local to latitude 54.8°N; Mercator scale varies with latitude. The route is not a navigation plan and does not provide a measured sailing distance, AIS position, forecast, or confirmed exposure.

Location coordinates (longitude, latitude): Kotka (26.95,60.47), Gdańsk (18.65,54.35), Toruń (18.60,53.01), Poznań (16.93,52.41).
